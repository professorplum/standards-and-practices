## For AI Agents

**Always check this section when:**
- A proposal involves collecting, storing, processing, or transmitting personal data.
- A proposal involves data retention, deletion, or cross-border data transfer.
- A proposal involves a new vendor or third-party service that will receive personal data.
- A proposal involves audit logging, access controls, or encryption.

When in doubt about a compliance question, flag it explicitly and request human review. Do not make compliance judgments autonomously.

## Maintenance

Compliance requirements change. When regulations are updated or new ones apply, update these documents and notify engineering leadership.
